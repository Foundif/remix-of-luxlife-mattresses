# Remix of Luxlife Mattresses

BUILD A PREMIUM SPORTS-FASHION ECOMMERCE BRAND WEBSITE

PROJECT

Build a premium, fully customized ecommerce website for a modern socks / sports-lifestyle brand.

This is a serious commercial website project with a target perceived value of ₹45,000+.

The website must NOT look like:

Shopify

WooCommerce templates

Generic ecommerce UI

Basic fashion store

Cheap startup website

AI-generated template

Simple product catalog

The visual ambition should be inspired by the confidence, energy, editorial art direction, product presentation and premium sports-fashion feel of brands such as Nike, Puma, Adidas and premium D2C fashion brands.

Do NOT copy their branding, logos, layouts, proprietary visuals or exact designs.

Instead, take inspiration from their design philosophy:

Strong visual storytelling + bold typography + premium photography + movement + confidence + simplicity + conversion.

The final result should feel like a brand that is ready to compete in a serious fashion/sports market.

1. THE MOST IMPORTANT PART — HERO SECTION

THE HERO MUST BE THE SHOWSTOPPER

Do NOT create a conventional ecommerce hero containing:

"Welcome to our store"

"Shop our socks"

and a simple image beside it.

Absolutely avoid that.

The hero should feel like the opening scene of a premium sports-fashion campaign.

Imagine a Nike/Puma campaign landing page, but for this socks brand.

Hero composition

Use a full-screen / near-full-screen cinematic hero.

Desktop:

100vh or approximately 90vh

Full-width visual

Strong foreground product/lifestyle photography

Dramatic composition

Large typography

Minimal navigation interference

Strong CTA

Subtle motion

Premium contrast

Editorial spacing

Mobile:

80–90vh

Carefully cropped portrait-oriented image

Typography adapted for narrow screens

CTA remains immediately accessible

Product remains visually dominant
